Chainsaw — one hit on civilians, two on police, more on armor. Fuel drains with every swing; empty fuel means slow, weak swings. Gasoline cans refill it.

Pistol — drops police in one shot at range but is weak against armor. Ammo is limited.

Explosives — orange-marked zones by condemned houses. Plant, run, watch the block go up. Everything caught in the blast counts.

Combo — kills chain while the timer holds. Higher chains multiply every point you score.
